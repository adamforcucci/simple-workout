## Full description (max 4000)

```
A no-nonsense gym tracker. Log your sets, run your rest timer, see your progress, and keep your data on your phone.

No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Nothing to sell.

LOG WORKOUTS FAST
• Routines with default weights, reps and rest times
• Supersets that move you straight to the next exercise, then back to the start of the round
• Last session's numbers beside every set, or flip to your best-ever set
• Warm-up, drop and failure sets
• Swipe a set away, swap an exercise, drag to reorder
• Edit a finished workout or repeat it later

REST TIMER THAT WORKS IN YOUR POCKET
• Starts when you tick a set
• Vibrates and beeps when rest is up, even with the screen off

HEART RATE
• Pairs with standard Bluetooth heart rate straps (tested with the Amazfit Helio Strap and Wahoo TICKR)
• Live heart rate and zone during your workout
• Keeps recording with the screen locked
• Heart rate graph, time in zones, and calories from heart rate

PROGRESS
• History calendar
• Weekly volume and workout counts
• Sets per muscle group
• Best lifts and estimated one-rep max trends for every exercise
• Body weight log

STRAVA (OPTIONAL)
• Connect your Strava account to upload workouts with your exercises, sets, heart rate and calories
• Strava shows them as Weight Training with its own workout log and muscle map

HEALTH CONNECT (OPTIONAL)
• Save finished workouts to Health Connect with sets, heart rate and calories
• Write-only: the app never reads your health data

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Everything is stored on your phone, not on our servers
• Nothing leaves your phone except to Strava, and only if you connect it
• Export a backup file whenever you like
• Light, dark or match-your-phone theme
• kg or lb

656 exercises built in, plus your own.
```
